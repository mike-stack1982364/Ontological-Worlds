How to Train

Mode 1 — Relational Conflict Matrix

Trial 1 establishes the initial relational world. Every later trial preserves controlled partial structure while changing one complete letter-role and one direction. Maximal relational interference and a balanced whole-triad Match/No Match distribution are fixed internally so users cannot weaken or bias the intended training. Compare Statements 1–3 with the trial N positions earlier, decide whether Statement 3 follows from the first two statements, and then judge the complete triad.

Keyboard mapping: Statement 1 A/S, Statement 2 D/F, Statement 3 H/J, within-trial entailment K/L, and complete-triad N-back Spacebar/N. In every pair, the left key is the positive response and the right key is the negative response.
