How to Train

Mode 1 — Relational Conflict Matrix

Choose 4, 8 or 16 compass directions before starting Mode 1. Four uses the cardinal directions, eight adds the diagonals, and sixteen adds the intermediate directions. Finer resolution increases directional precision. Cognitive interference changes lure difficulty independently of compass resolution.

Response Window (seconds per trial) has been removed. Each trial remains available until all five relevant decisions are entered. Immediate feedback is given after each input, and the next premise appears only after all five decisions have been completed.

For the first N trials there is no historical N-back target. Therefore Statements 1, 2 and 3 and the complete triad are always No Match during that warm-up period. The entailment question remains independent: it asks only whether Statement 3 logically follows from Statements 1 and 2 in the current trial.

After warm-up, answer whether each displayed statement matches its counterpart in the trial N positions earlier, whether Statement 3 is entailed by Statements 1 and 2, and whether the complete triad matches. Statement matching uses one globally consistent mapping of roles; reversed wording counts only when the compass direction is inverted correctly.

Sequential continuity is mandatory. Relative to the immediately preceding trial, every new trial retains one or two letters and changes one or two; it also retains one or two statement directions and changes one or two. This prevents both exact repetition and unrelated replacement. The N-back target still determines the five correct answers, while the immediately preceding trial controls cognitive continuity.

Keyboard mapping: Statement 1 A/S, Statement 2 D/F, Statement 3 H/J, within-trial entailment K/L, and complete-triad N-back Spacebar/N. In every pair, the left key is the positive response and the right key is the negative response. G is deliberately unused.
