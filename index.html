How to Train

Mode 1 — Relational Conflict Matrix

After the first N memory-fill trials, answer five questions on every trial: whether each of the three statements matches a statement in the trial N positions earlier, whether Statement 3 is logically entailed by Statements 1 and 2, and whether the complete three-statement structure matches. Statement matches require one globally consistent mapping of letters and one-to-one statement assignment. Reversed wording counts only when the compass direction is inverted correctly. A single or partial match never makes the whole triad a match.

The cognitive-interference control progressively emphasises adjacent 16-direction substitutions, repeated letters with changed roles, and two-of-three lures. Ontological and Inner/Outer labels remain irrelevant to Mode 1 scoring.

Mode 2 — Ontological Integration

Compare the complete relational structure of the current ontology trial with the trial exactly N positions back. Ontology categories and Inner/Outer labels are visible transformations only and do not determine the answer.
