Promising components. An experimental combination.

The papers study particular reasoning, memory and learning tasks. They do not test this exact game. Improvements on practised tasks and closely related tasks are better established than broad gains in intelligence. The custom ontology, its facets and the combined training method remain proposals to test.

Connect the research to your practice

What each mode asks you to do

01

Mode 1 · Relational reasoning

Use the Da Vinci cross-domain method to build and compare relationships in different imagined worlds, keeping the supplied compass relationships intact. Give five answers: three statement matches, whether the conclusion follows, and whether the complete triad matches the correct earlier trial.

  • Integrate several relationships.
  • Separate a valid conclusion from a familiar one.
  • Track roles through changed letters and wording.
Read Mode 1 research →

02

Mode 2 · Ontological integration

Keep categories and perspectives attached to the correct ends of each relationship. More advanced variations ask you to coordinate different facets of one entity and structures inside larger worlds.

  • Preserve category–perspective–role bindings.
  • Align complete structures across trials.
  • Coordinate relationships at more than one level.
Read Mode 2 research →

N

Other training · Ordered N-back

Listen to or view digits, update the remembered sequence, and recognise a number only when it returns in the same position exactly N trials later.

  • Update information as each new trial arrives.
  • Keep item identity tied to sequence position.
  • Reject familiar digits in the wrong place or trial.
Read N-back research →

These are design connections. A study of a similar component does not establish the effect of the whole mode, and the ordered-digit variation is not identical to laboratory dual N-back.

Turn the evidence into a useful exercise

What this suggests for practice

These are proposed applications of the research to this game. Each example keeps the actual scoring rules intact while making the thinking more deliberate.

01

Build the map, then test the claim

At 8 directions, suppose A is north of B and B is east of C. Following the two equal steps puts A northeast of C. Imagine the letters as stations in a transport network, then as planets in a solar system. Preserve the same relationships in both worlds.

Now imagine the candidate says A is west of C. It is still false even if the same wrong claim appeared earlier. Check the map and the memory separately.

Reasoning and model practice

02

Make a category into a working role

For Outer Connection H, imagine H as a station connected to another station; then as a cell connected to another cell. The connected-member role stays, although its embodiment changes.

Carry over every other endpoint job and compass direction too. If H has a second facet, it is the same entity doing another job. Explain what stays fixed before calling the whole world a match.

Comparison and role-based categories

N

Remember the slot and the right round

At 2-back, imagine round 1 is [3, 8], round 2 is [6, 2], and round 3 is [3, 5]. Compare round 3 with round 1: only the first position matches.

For the next round, round 2 becomes the target. Ask “Which number, in which slot, in which round?” A familiar number alone does not answer that question.

Shared practice

The Da Vinci / World Building method

Give the symbols meaning, then carry the relationships into a different domain. A useful imagined world must obey the supplied constraints.

  1. Build. Give each letter a concrete role. Imagine one coherent scene in which the required relationships hold.
  2. Explain. Identify what depends on what, which direction the relationship runs, and which detail would change the answer.
  3. Transfer. Rebuild the same structure using distant subject matter—for example, an ecosystem, a transport network or a team.
  4. Check. Keep the same roles and relationships. Name a point where your analogy would stop working.

Preserve the relationship through the change

A branching river and a distribution network can both carry something from one source to several destinations. The objects change; the one-to-many flow can remain. A loop that returns to its source has a different structure, even if it uses the same objects.

For a spatial trial, your imagined explanation must also retain the exact supplied directions. An interesting story earns no exemption from a conflicting clue.

If you add a causal mechanism, state it as a rule of your imagined world. Compass positions alone do not supply that mechanism. The game scores the written pattern; use these checks to assess your own imagined scene.

Research on analogy, generative learning and relational integration motivates these steps. It does not yet establish that this named combination, or this specific category system, increases general intelligence.
